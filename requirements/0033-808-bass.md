# R-0033 — 808 bass: a sine sub that glides and cracks, under the trap style

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Milestone:** M7
- **Owner:** Gustavo Delgadillo (see project-specifics.md)
- **Created:** 2026-09-27
- **Depends on:** R-0007 (synth + distortion), R-0009 (beat builder), R-0026
  (genre presets), R-0042 (sing over a style)
- **Realized by:** SPEC-0033
- **QA:** `qa` agent run scoped to this requirement

## 1. Statement

The engine must have an **808 bass**: a sine sub-bass at a note's pitch, with a
long decay, a **glide** between notes that overlap, and a **drive** that
saturates it until it cracks in the mids. Given a list of notes, it renders a
bass part of an exact length, deterministic and bounded.

The first place it plays is **singing over trap** (R-0042). There, an 808 hits on
every kick of the trap pattern, at the root of what was sung, down in the 808's
register. It arrives as its own track next to the voice and the drums, and is
played, saved and exported with them.

The owner asked for it in their own words: *"arranca con el bajo 808"*. The
north-star prompt (R-0026 AC6) already asks for it: *"El bajo: un 808 largo con
distorsión hasta que cruje."*

## 2. Rationale

Trap without an 808 is only half a trap. The style presets (R-0026) already
describe the drums, but nothing plays the low end, so a take sung over "trap" sits
on hats and a kick with nothing under the voice.

The 808 is also the first **pitched accompaniment voice**. It is where "a track
that fits my melody" starts: a bass that sits on the song's root now, and that
the melody-fitting harmony (planned next) will move from chord to chord. That is
why the glide is built and tested now, even though a bass on one root never
slides.

## 3. Acceptance criteria

- **AC1 — A sine at the note's pitch.** With no drive, each note sounds as a sine
  at its frequency: the energy at the note's frequency dominates its harmonics.
  One oscillator runs through the whole part (the phase never jumps between
  notes).
- **AC2 — Glide on overlapping notes only.** When a note starts *before the
  previous one has ended* (legato), the pitch travels from where the voice is at
  that moment to the new note's frequency over the glide time, linearly in
  cents: halfway through, it sits at their geometric mean. The amplitude does
  not re-attack, so there is no second thump. A note that starts after the
  previous one ended starts on its own pitch with a fresh attack. Touching
  counts as not overlapping.
- **AC3 — A long, click-free envelope.** A phrase is a fresh note plus the
  legato notes chained to it. Each phrase starts from silence (its first sample
  is 0), attacks in 2 ms, decays exponentially with the configured decay time,
  and releases to silence over the last 5 ms before it ends (or before the part
  ends, whichever comes first). No clicks: over every attack, every release and
  every join, for notes at or above 30 Hz (the 808 plays in `[40, 80)`), no
  sample-to-sample step exceeds **3×** the steepest step of a sustained note at
  the highest pitch involved **and the same drive** (at drive 0, a sine). A hard cut with no release must fail that check,
  so the check cannot pass vacuously.
- **AC4 — Drive cracks it.** `drive` in `0..=1`. At `0` the part is a clean sine
  (odd harmonics near silent). As drive rises, the 3rd harmonic's share of the
  energy never falls, and at `1` it is clearly audible. Every sample stays in
  `[-1, 1]` at any drive.
- **AC5 — Total and deterministic.** The same input always renders the same
  samples, whatever order the notes arrive in. The output has exactly the
  requested length, except that a zero sample rate gives an empty part. The
  renderer never panics:
  - no notes → silence;
  - a note with a non-finite frequency, onset or duration → skipped;
  - a note whose frequency or duration is zero or negative, or whose onset is
    negative → skipped (an onset of 0 plays);
  - a note at or above Nyquist → skipped;
  - a note that starts at or past the end → skipped;
  - a note that runs past the end → cut there, with its release (#71's lesson);
  - a glide, decay or drive setting that is not a usable number → a defined
    fallback, never a NaN or a sample outside `[-1, 1]`.

  When two notes start on the same sample, the voice plays one of them, chosen
  by a fixed rule, not by list order.
- **AC6 — Trap brings an 808.** A style's plan says whether it has a bass. The
  trap preset has an 808; corrido, metal and free have none. A plan saved before
  this field existed still reads, as "no bass".
- **AC7 — Singing over trap plays it under you.** When the owner sings over trap:
  - **Its own track.** The accompaniment carries a bass part next to the voice
    and drums, with the same sample rate, the same length and the same whole
    bars.
  - **On the kick.** Every 808 note starts on the exact sample a kick starts on,
    and lasts until the next kick (the last one until the loop ends).
  - **At your root.** It plays the root of the grid the voice's notes are shown
    on (the take's followed root, or the default root when none was followed),
    moved by whole octaves (2:1) into `[40, 80)` Hz.
  - **With the style's drive.** It uses the plan's drive.

  Over any other style the accompaniment has no bass part.
- **AC8 — Played, saved, exported, without clipping.** The studio plays the
  bass part with the voice and drums from one play button at one scheduled
  instant. Saving writes it as its own stem and exporting mixes it into the
  master. **Over any style, what the studio plays is what export writes:** each
  track at its export level, and all of them scaled down together only when
  their sum would pass full scale (owner decision). This also ends the clipping
  that voice + drums already had under corrido, metal and free.
- **AC9 — Tests, docs, gates.** Every public item is documented. All four
  toolchain gates are green.

## 4. Constraints & non-goals

- **Real-time discipline does not apply yet.** Like the other renderers, this
  one renders offline into a buffer. It is not on the audio callback.
- **One pitch per trap part.** The bass plays the root only. Moving it with the
  harmony belongs to the melody-fitting harmony requirement. The glide exists
  now for that.
- **No 808 outside trap.** It does not play under corrido, metal or free, in hum
  mode or in describe → music (R-0027). The other styles get their own bass
  voices (tololoche, tuba) with the genre cards.
- **No new UI controls.** No 808 knob, no mute, no level slider. The studio view
  that follows R-0033 gives tracks their controls.
- **No kick changes.** The trap kick keeps its sound. The 808 sits with it, as
  trap producers layer them.

## 6. Decision log

| Date | Decision | Rationale |
|------|----------|-----------|
| 2026-09-27 | The 808 hits on every kick, at the root (owner: "sigue al bombo") | That is how trap is made: the 808 doubles the kick's rhythm. A held note per bar would sound like a pad, not an 808. |
| 2026-09-27 | Glide only when notes overlap (owner: "al encadenar notas") | This is how a producer's 808 slides: overlapping notes slide, separate notes hit fresh. Every note falling from above would turn each hit into a kick. |
| 2026-09-27 | Its own track, not mixed into the drums | The studio view that follows gives each track its own lane, and a bass baked into the drums could not be moved or muted there. |
| 2026-09-27 | Drive comes from the plan's drive, not from a separate knob | A description's "808 … con distorsión hasta que cruje" already sets `drive` (R-0025). One number, one meaning. |
| 2026-09-27 | **Driven by default** (owner: "sí, saturado por defecto") | The style chips send no description, so the plan's drive is the intent default, 0.40: about −13 dB of third harmonic. Trap 808s usually carry that grit so they read on small speakers. To be tuned by ear. |
| 2026-09-27 | **One master gain, mirroring export** (owner: "ganancia maestra, como el export") | Voice at −1 dBFS, drums at full scale and a sustained 808 sum past full scale and clip the output device, which is ugly and not the crack anyone asked for. Export already scales the whole mix down only when it would clip, so playback does the same, with the same levels. |
| 2026-09-27 | AC2, AC3 and AC5 made precise (architect design review, two rounds) | Four fixes: the glide starts from the voice's current pitch, since a note cut mid-glide is not at its target. The envelope belongs to the phrase, so a short legato note cannot click. The click threshold is 3× at the highest pitch involved, measured, because 1× fails a 2 ms attack at 40 Hz; it holds from about 28 Hz up (a 2 ms attack is √((79.6/f)² + 1)× at any rate), so AC3 is scoped to notes at or above 30 Hz. Onset 0 plays, ties are broken by rule, and a zero rate gives an empty part. |
